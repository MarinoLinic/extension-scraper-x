Settings

Every captured batch is written to the local archive database before the page scrolls — that checkpoint is always on and is what survives tab reloads and browser restarts. The options below control pacing, limits, exports, filenames, and optional media downloads.

Most reliable background setup

  1. Drag the scraping tab into its own Chrome window.
  2. Keep that tab selected.
  3. Do not minimize the window.
  4. Keep at least part of the window visible.
  5. Browse normally in a different Chrome window.

The “Continue in hidden tabs” setting is best effort; Chrome may throttle timers and X may stop rendering new posts.
