Reliable long-run setup
  1. Drag the scraping tab into its own Chrome window.
  2. Keep that tab selected.
  3. Do not minimize the window.
  4. Keep at least part of the window visible.
  5. Browse normally in a different Chrome window.

Hidden-tab mode is best effort because Chrome and X may throttle loading.
